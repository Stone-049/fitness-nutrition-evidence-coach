# Fitness Nutrition Evidence Coach

An evidence-aware fitness and sports-nutrition Agent Skill for ordinary adults, with a primary focus on Chinese-speaking users.

It combines training programming, nutrition guidance, red-flag screening, sourced evidence, de-identified community case prototypes, image-analysis safeguards, and question-bank evaluation.

## Highlights

- 31 indexed guidelines, systematic reviews, meta-analyses, and key RCTs
- 20 de-identified community case prototypes
- Long-term coaching profile and decision-log design
- Evidence tiers and data-quality rules
- Safety and referral boundaries
- Question-bank validation and blind scoring scripts
- Local-first privacy posture

## Quick start

Copy `skills/fitness-coach/` into your agent's skills directory, or ask the agent to load its `SKILL.md`.

```text
Use skills/fitness-coach to design a three-day beginner program for an office worker.
```

## Scope

This project is for general health education and training planning. It is not medical diagnosis, treatment, physical therapy, or clinical nutrition care. Read `DISCLAIMER.md` and `PRIVACY.md` before use.
