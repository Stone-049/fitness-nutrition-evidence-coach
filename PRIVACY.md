# Privacy

The Skill is designed for local-first use.

- Do not commit user profiles, training logs, nutrition logs, health metrics,
  body images, wearable exports, or coaching transcripts.
- Body-image analysis requires adult confirmation, explicit consent, identity
  minimization, non-sexual content, and preferably opaque athletic clothing.
- Do not upload nude, sexualized, exploitative, or minor-related images.
- Delete temporary analysis files when they are no longer needed.
- Use synthetic or fully de-identified data in issues, tests, and examples.

The maintainers cannot control data stored by third-party AI providers,
wearable platforms, or chat applications. Review those services separately.
